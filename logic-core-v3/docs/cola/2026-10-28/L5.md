# L5 · Reviews, the CTA, the footer and the FAQ

Model: **Opus 5.5, high effort**. Budget: ~4 h. Requests: items 70 and 71 of `REVISION-3.md`. Screenshots `lote2/08`–`19`.

## Items
1. **The review ring:**
   - `?resenas=scroll` becomes the **default** (Valentino prefers scroll; `gatillo` stays as a flag).
   - Disable text selection on the ring and its cards (dragging was selecting text in blue, `lote2/08`).
   - A card seen from behind shows a **plain white back** (no mirrored text), and cards never overlap the edge of their neighbour (`lote2/09`).
   - The hidden CTA glow shows in front of the logo (`lote2/10`): it must never draw over the logo.
2. **Leaving the reviews:** while scrolling out, the camera keeps the reviews in view, **looking diagonally from above, until they are fully stored**, and only then pulls away. The step from the reviews to the CTA **is a trigger (gatillo)**, at a readable speed. The floor opening is orderly (`lote2/11`, `12`): the floor opens, the reviews go down, the floor closes — the floor never cuts through the reviews.
3. **Reviews on the phone/tablet** (`lote2/13`): whole cards that enter from the right and leave on the left, **always complete** (the screen edge is the limit; no card cut by the sides). The step to the CTA is also a trigger here, not too fast, so the text can be read.
4. **Footer → FAQ on desktop:**
   - The «Scrolleá una vez más…» button lower and its sentence on **one line** (`lote2/14`).
   - During the swap the sunk boxes never stay floating in the air (`lote2/15`).
   - The questions block: «Preguntas frecuentes», then a short intro — «Lo que más nos preguntan antes de arrancar. Si no está acá, escribinos.» — then the questions, so the block has no big empty space at the bottom (`lote2/16`, `17`).
   - «Tocá una pregunta y mirá lo que pasa.» centered in its block (`lote2/18`).
   - The answer block's storing animation is **exactly the form's**: the whole black block sinks, nothing else; no black residue on the corners (`lote2/19`).
   - The FAQ's WhatsApp button uses the same component and effect as the footer's «Escribinos por WhatsApp».
5. **Footer and FAQ on the phone:** the order is inverted: **FAQ first, then the footer** (more intuitive there; the background animation doesn't matter much on the phone).
