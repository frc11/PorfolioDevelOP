3. **The preloader without arrows:** remove the dashed arrows. Instead, the **sound button appears in its place and emits a vibration that crosses the whole background** — a thin but visible line (a ripple) that starts at the button so people follow the wave and notice it. If loading takes longer, it vibrates several times **with a heartbeat rhythm** ("tu-tu … tu-tu …", two close pulses, then a pause). On the phone/tablet both buttons (sound and movement) vibrate, no arrows. Movement stays **beta**: the button and permission flow stay as they are. Fix also the report defect: movement turned on doesn't come back on after a reload (in `retomarElMovimiento`, try `DeviceOrientationEvent.requestPermission()` without a gesture first; if granted, turn it on; if rejected, wait for the tap as today).
